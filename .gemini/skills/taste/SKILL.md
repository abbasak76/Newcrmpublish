# Taste Skill (Modern Aesthetics & Refinement)

Enforces modern design sensibilities, clean typographic hierarchy, balanced whitespace, and restrained color palettes.

## Rules
1. Avoid unstyled harsh borders; use `border-border/80` or `border-border/50`.
2. Ensure generous touch targets (`min-h-[44px]` or `py-2.5 px-3.5`).
3. Maintain strict hierarchy with Vazirmatn Persian typography.
